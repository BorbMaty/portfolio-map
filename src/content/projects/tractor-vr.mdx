## What I built

TODO(Mara): write in your own words

Raw notes:

- VR tractor simulator of a John Deere 6195M for Meta Quest 2.
- Implements: plough, front mower, front weight, tipping trailer; three-point hitch, PTO.
- Cab with grabbable steering wheel and levers, pressable switches, hand tracking.
- 2 × 2 km world near Reghin, built from Blender assets generated by Python scripts (Full PC and Light Quest versions).
- Tutorial: 9 self-checking lessons with 1–5 star ratings.
- Three builds from one project: Quest standalone, PC VR over Link, PC desktop.
- University project, Sapientia (IRUM).
